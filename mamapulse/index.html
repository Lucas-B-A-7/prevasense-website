MamaPulse

Wrist-worn pulse wave velocity monitoring for continuous, independent pre-eclampsia risk assessment

MamaPulse is a wearable, non-invasive device being developed to support continuous, independent assessment of pre-eclampsia risk in pregnant women.

How It Works

  • Two PPG sensors positioned under velcro straps on the wrist, spaced a fixed 60 mm apart
  • Sensors detect the pulse waveform at each site; onboard firmware calculates pulse wave velocity from the transit time between the two pulses
  • Result shown on an OLED screen, which also displays a history of past readings
  • An onboard LED gives a simple Green, Yellow, or Red risk level

Designed for use by pregnant women at home, MamaPulse aims to make pre-eclampsia risk monitoring accessible without the cost or distance of a clinic visit, while also giving clinicians a longitudinal risk history at a patient's first appointment.

The device is currently at the functional-prototype stage: it has passed benchtop testing for battery life, drop durability, weight, compactness, electrical safety, data storage, and ease of use, but its pulse-wave-velocity algorithm cannot yet reliably calculate risk accurately enough for clinical screening, and MamaPulse has not yet been clinically validated.
