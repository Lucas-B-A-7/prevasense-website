Our Mission

Reducing the toll of pre-eclampsia on mothers and babies in Uganda.

PrevaSense exists to close the early-detection gap that lets pre-eclampsia go unnoticed until it becomes an emergency. We design low-cost, non-invasive monitoring devices — built and tested alongside clinicians and communities in Uganda — that let women track their risk over the course of a pregnancy, flag warning signs early, and get to care in time.

Our goal is simple: fewer maternal complications and deaths, fewer fetal and neonatal complications and deaths, and more pregnant women who feel informed and in control of their own care.
