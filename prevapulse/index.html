PrevaPulse

Portable, non-invasive pulse wave velocity monitoring to support early pre-eclampsia risk assessment

PrevaPulse is a portable, non-invasive device being developed to support early assessment of pre-eclampsia risk in pregnant women.

How It Works

  • Two separate, unequal wraps positioned over the brachial and radial arteries on the same arm
  • Force-sensitive resistor (FSR) sensors detect the pulse wave at both sites
  • The processing unit calculates pulse wave velocity from the pulse transit time across a fixed distance of 150 mm
  • Result shown on an OLED screen, with green, yellow, and red LEDs giving a simple three-level risk indication

Designed for use by nurses and midwives, PrevaPulse aims to make arterial-stiffness assessment more accessible in antenatal clinics and low-resource healthcare settings.

The device is currently at the functional-prototype stage and has undergone preliminary benchtop testing but has not yet been clinically validated.
